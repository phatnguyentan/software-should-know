# Founder Roadmap theo Domain Knowledge System

Nếu chuyển từ engineer sang founder, trọng tâm chuyển từ **xây hệ thống** sang **xây công ty tạo value**.

## 1) Problem & Customer
- Problem discovery
- Customer discovery
- Value hypothesis
- Product-market fit signals

## 2) Product & Technology Strategy
- Product strategy
- Technology strategy (Build/Buy/Partner)
- Platform vs Product decisions
- AI strategy and AI governance baseline

## 3) Go-to-Market & Revenue
- ICP/segment focus
- Pricing and packaging
- Distribution channels
- Sales motion

## 4) Finance & Operations
- Unit economics
- Burn rate and runway
- Budgeting and allocation
- Operating system for execution

## 5) Team & Leadership
- Hiring core team
- Culture and principles
- Decision quality under uncertainty
- Organizational design for scale

## Founder Leverage Ladder
```
Write code
  ↓
Design systems
  ↓
Design processes
  ↓
Enable teams
  ↓
Shape strategy
  ↓
Build company
```
