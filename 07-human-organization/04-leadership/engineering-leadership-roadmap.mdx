# Engineering Leadership Roadmap

Tài liệu này chuẩn hóa roadmap leadership từ bộ interview/practice cũ sang cấu trúc canonical.

## 1. Technical Leadership
- Technical vision
- Architecture decision making under constraints
- Quality/reliability standards
- Technical strategy and trade-offs

## 2. Team & Organization
- Team topology and ownership
- Mentoring/coaching
- Conflict resolution
- Hiring and capability building

## 3. Delivery & Operations
- Prioritization and planning
- Risk management
- Incident leadership
- Cross-team execution

## 4. Communication
- Technical writing (RFC/ADR)
- Stakeholder communication
- Executive communication
- Decision transparency

## 5. Business Alignment
- Product impact awareness
- Cost/performance trade-offs
- Build/Buy/Partner decisions
- Organizational leverage

## Interview Focus Mapping
- **Senior+**: technical depth + system trade-off judgment
- **Staff/Principal**: cross-team architecture and influence
- **EM/Head**: people system, execution system, business alignment
